Privacy Policy

Veda Associates · Spa, Aesthetics and Pharmacy · Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy explains how Veda Associates ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects the personal information of our clients, including when we contact you on WhatsApp.

Information we collect

  • Name, mobile number, email address, address, date of birth and anniversary.
  • Appointment, consultation, treatment, prescription and session records.
  • Invoices, quotations, receipts and payment records.
  • Messages you send to us on WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • To provide our spa, aesthetic and pharmacy services and keep your treatment records.
  • To send you invoices, quotations, receipts, prescriptions and aftercare information on WhatsApp.
  • To send appointment-related messages and birthday wishes.
  • To reply to your questions.

WhatsApp messages

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (provided by Meta) to send and receive messages. Your mobile number and the message content are processed by Meta to deliver these messages, under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. You can stop receiving our WhatsApp messages at any time by replying STOP or by telling our staff.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (such as hosting and WhatsApp messaging), or when required by law.

Security and retention

Your records are stored on secured servers and can be accessed only by authorised staff. We keep records for as long as needed to provide care and to meet legal and medical record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop WhatsApp messages, by contacting us at our clinic or by replying to any of our WhatsApp messages.
